Alphabet Safari helps little learners, ages 2 to 5, meet the English alphabet and the Arabic alphabet, with Leo the Lion and Noor the Bunny as friendly guides.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
• Explore: big, bright letters, each with a picture word (A for Apple, ز for زَرَافَة, giraffe) and a clear recorded letter sound.
• Trace: draw each letter with a finger in rainbow sparkles. A well-traced letter earns stars.
• Pop Balloons: hear a letter and pop the balloons that carry it.
• Find Letter: which letter does the word start with?
• Memory Cards: match each letter with its picture.
• Sticker Park: stars unlock stickers to place in a magic park.

MADE FOR BOTH ALPHABETS
• 26 English letters, in upper and lower case.
• 28 Arabic letters, with the harakat (fatha, damma, kasra, sukun) and optional English transliteration.
• The whole app switches between English and Arabic, including the right-to-left layout.
• Choose a boy or a girl explorer; Arabic praise uses the right form for each.

GENTLE AND SAFE
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking.
• Works fully offline: the Android app has no internet access at all.
• A grown-ups-only question protects Parents Corner (speech speed, sounds, resets and the privacy policy).
• Full-screen play keeps small fingers in the app, and Back closes a dialog before it leaves.

FOR PARENTS
Your child's name is optional. It, the stars and the stickers stay on this device and can be erased from Parents Corner at any time. Phrases with your child's name are only spoken by voices that run on the device.
